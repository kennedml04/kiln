# Visualize

A visual plus stated numbers is the answer. Don't ask the person to imagine anything.

Offering when not asked: one line ("Want me to render that?"), once per topic. Never for a single simple dimension.

Every visual:
1. **Orientation first.** Name the sides in the person's words (top/bottom, wall side/room side) and use those names on every label.
2. **Look along the feature** being discussed (down the screw axis, a section through the channel), not only an isometric view.
3. **Add a size reference** the person knows (a hand, a coin, the board it mounts to).
4. **Units:** printed parts in mm; rooms and woodwork in the units the person uses, with mm alongside.
5. **Say it in words too:** the one or two facts the picture shows ("the lid clears the connector by 3 mm").

How: render the exported STL from `out/` to images (front, side, top and one along the feature), or write one self-contained HTML page with an interactive 3D view. Keep renders in `out/`, which git ignores; share them only when the person asks.
